About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Chemistry at Government Engineering College, Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India, and a Ph.D. Research Scholar in Environmental Chemistry under the supervision of Dr. Vikas Kumar Jain.

My research focuses on groundwater quality assessment, hydrogeochemistry, heavy-metal contamination, GIS-based environmental analysis, water quality indices, and human health-risk assessment.
